No installation needed. Upload data in the template format for each tool below.

Method & limitations

  • Every procedure here is a from-scratch JavaScript port of the corresponding function in the DIFair Python package, checked against its actual output on test data. Mantel–Haenszel, standardization, Breslow–Day, generalized Mantel–Haenszel, all fairness metrics, and the survey/jackknife functions match to 5+ decimal places. Logistic-regression-based procedures (item-level logistic DIF, ordinal logistic DIF, pipeline attribution) fit their own solver rather than statsmodels/scikit-learn's, so results are very close but not bit-identical, especially for pipeline attribution under sample reweighting.
  • Being flagged for DIF or a fairness gap statistically doesn't automatically mean "unfair" — flagged items and gaps need expert content and context review.
  • All tools here support two-group comparisons only, matching the underlying package. With a small sample (fewer than roughly 50 people), statistical power is limited and results should be treated as indicative only.
